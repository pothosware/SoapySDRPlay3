Unreleased
==========================

- True dual channel operation for the RSPduo in Dual Tuner mode: one stream
  carrying both tuners, sample aligned, and per channel control of gain, AGC,
  antenna, bandwidth and the tuner settings

- Slots are cut at multiples of the slot size rather than wherever a ring
  happens to fill. The old rule held only while both tuners were handed
  identically sized packets; when they were not, the two rings cut in different
  places and stayed apart, which readStream() could only answer by draining both
  and returning SOAPY_SDR_OVERFLOW. A packet that straddles a boundary is now
  split across the two slots instead of going wholly into one, and a ring that
  has just been drained waits for a boundary before it starts filling again, so
  both tuners always resume on the same slot.

- Smaller slots: 20480 frames before decimation instead of 65536, and 24 of them
  instead of 8. A slot is what a reader waits for after a drain, and 33 ms of it
  was nearly three blocks for an application asking for 12.5 ms at a time. The
  ring stays about a quarter of a second deep.
